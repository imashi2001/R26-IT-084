## Hard-cover lettering (if required by your stream)

Per SLIIT guidelines, hard-bound cover typically carries:

1. Full thesis title  
2. Candidate name(s)  
3. Degree sought  
4. Year  

Spine: short title · name · degree · year  

Confirm colour/binding with **your degree’s** current instruction sheet (undergraduate research projects often follow the Faculty research report template issued for IT4200 — ask your supervisor for the exact Annex 03 specimen used this year).

## Recommended document front-matter order

1. Title page (above)  
2. Declaration (signed)  
3. Acknowledgement  
4. Abstract  
5. Table of Contents  
6. List of Tables  
7. List of Figures  
8. List of Plates (hardware photos)  
9. List of Abbreviations  
10. Chapters  
11. References (IEEE or faculty-mandated style)  
12. Appendices (metrics verification, latency raw data, wiring photos)
